FIRST EXPERIMENT

From images to answers

  1. Input: open an image folder, a video or a camera. A demonstration is a good first check.
  2. Mask: keep the specimen and add holes. Apply once; the entire selected ROI stays requested.
  3. Solve: use the preset first. Play / Pause controls acquisition. Fixed and consecutive references measure different things.
  4. Results: inspect one field or enable recording. Probe points, fit strain over a chosen window, create a line or gauge, then export the computed values and recipe.

Space: Play / Pause. R: capture reference. Esc: clear measurement selection. Shortcuts are inactive while editing a form or mask.
