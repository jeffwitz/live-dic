live-dic

Python-supervised digital image correlation, built toward qualified 4K at 60 FPS.

Foundational work by Pierre Margerit. His tests_gpuio project established the original DIC prototype; his later Strobox repository contains the Three.js/WebGPU camera prototype that informed this browser path.
